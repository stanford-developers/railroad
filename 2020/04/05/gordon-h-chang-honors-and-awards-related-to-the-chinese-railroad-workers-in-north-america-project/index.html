Gordon H. Chang – Honors and Awards Related to the Chinese Railroad Workers In North America Project

April 5, 2020

Gordon H. Chang – Receives Honors and Awards for His Achievement Related to the History of the Chinese Railroad Workers in North America.

Selected list of honors and awards — 

Committee of 100 Award for Gordon H. Chang

California Legislature; Assembly. Certificate of Recognition. Professor Gordon H. Chang. 2019 APA Heritage Awards and Reception. This certificate is presented to Professor Gordon H. Chang in recognition of your efforts to highly the contributions of Chinese railroad workers to the completion of the Transcontinental Railroad. The California Legislature applauds your dedication to commemorating the Chinese immigrants that built the first Transcontinental Railroad and thanks you for your tireless efforts to empower the next generation of Asian and Pacific Islander American leaders in the Bay Area.  Presented by David Chiu, 17th Assembly District.  May 1, 2019

Member of Congress, United States House of America. Certificate of Special Congressional Recognition.  Presented to Professor Gordon H. Chang; Chinese Railroad Workers in North America Project, in recognition of outstanding and invaluable service to the community.  Presented by Member of the U.S. Congress Nancy Pelosi. May 1, 2019

Member of Congress, United States of America. Certificate of Special Congressional Recognition.  Presented to Gordon Chang, in recognition of outstanding and invaluable service to the community.  Anna G. Eshoo, Member of Congress.  May 23, 2019

Member of Congress, United States of America. Certificate of Special Congressional Recognition. Presented to Gordon Chang. In Recognition of contributing to the restoration of the history of 19th century Chinese transcontinental Railroad workers, as well as for a dedicated commitment to the to the greater Silicon Valley Community. Ro Khanna, Member of the U.S. Congress Ro Khanna.  May 23, 2019

California State Assembly Commendation.  Presented by Evan Low, May 23, 2019

California Legislature; Assembly. Certificate of Recognition. On behalf of the California State Assembly, I, Assemblymember, Kansen Chu, on this 23rd day of May 2019, do hereby recognize Professor Gordon Chang, for his dedicated efforts in community to preserve and promote the history of the Chinese railroad workers who completed the Transcontinental Railroad during the 19th century. Thank you for all of your work!  Kansen Chu, Assemblymember, 25th District.

State of California; Senate. Certificate of Recognition. The California State Legislature is proud to recognize Gordon H. Chang, for your valuable work in restoring the history of the Chinese railroad workers in the 19th Century on the Transcontinental Railroad; properly preserving and remembers the history for future generations. Presented by Jerry Hill, State Senator, District 13, and Jim Beall, State Senator, District 15.  May 23, 2019

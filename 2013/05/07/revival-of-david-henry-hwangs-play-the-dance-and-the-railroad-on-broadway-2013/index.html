The Dance and the Railroad

On a California mountaintop in 1867 near the Transcontinental Railroad, two Chinese workers struggle through poverty and hunger to reconnect with the traditions of their homeland…

We are grateful to the Signature Theatre for making available this trailer and director’s interview for the production of “The Dance and the Railroad” that they mounted February 5 to March 24, 2013.

THE DANCE AND THE RAILROAD follows the twice-extended GOLDEN CHILD as the second production in the Residency One: David Henry Hwang Series.
